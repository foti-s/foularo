Fuel Prices Cyprus

Live fuel prices at every petrol station in Cyprus. Unleaded 95 and 98, diesel, heating oil and kerosene, on a list and a map, with price history. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Τιμές καυσίμων σε κάθε πρατήριο της Κύπρου, σε λίστα και χάρτη, με ιστορικό τιμών. Χωρίς λογαριασμό, διαφημίσεις ή παρακολούθηση.

Android app, coming soon to Google Play.

Privacy policy · Πολιτική απορρήτου

Prices come from the Retail Fuel Price Observatory of the Consumer Protection Service (Ministry of Energy, Commerce and Industry), via CY Connect. Foularo is an independent app and is not affiliated with the Government of Cyprus or with any fuel company.
